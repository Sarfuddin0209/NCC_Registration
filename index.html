About NCC

An opportunity to develop discipline, leadership, confidence and patriotism.

Developing Responsible Citizens

The National Cadet Corps is a youth development organisation that provides military training, discipline and leadership opportunities to students.

NCC encourages young people to become responsible citizens and contribute positively to society.

  • Personality development
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Discipline and responsibility
  • Adventure and outdoor activities
  • National integration

NCC History

Important milestones in the development of the National Cadet Corps.

1917 – University Officers Training Corps

The early foundation of organised student military training in India was established.

1948 – Establishment of NCC

The National Cadet Corps was established in India after independence.

1963 – Expansion of Training

NCC training and activities expanded to support national service and youth development.

Today – Youth Development

NCC continues to develop leadership, discipline, unity and service among students.

NCC Certificates

NCC certificate levels help cadets demonstrate their training and participation.

A

Certificate A

Generally associated with junior division or junior wing NCC training, subject to applicable rules.

B

Certificate B

Awarded to eligible cadets who complete the prescribed training and examination requirements.

C

Certificate C

An advanced NCC certificate awarded to eligible senior division or senior wing cadets.

Benefits of Joining NCC

NCC provides valuable opportunities for defence careers, employment, leadership, adventure and personal development.

🎖️

Defence Career Opportunities

NCC training and the NCC 'C' Certificate can provide eligible cadets with opportunities for special entry routes and SSB-based selection in the Armed Forces, subject to the applicable recruitment rules.

  • Special entry opportunities
  • SSB interview opportunities
  • Opportunities in Army, Navy and Air Force
  • Recruitment benefits where officially applicable
💼

Government & Job Benefits

NCC certificates and training may provide additional weightage, preference or other recruitment benefits in certain government and uniformed-service recruitments, depending on the official notification.

  • Recruitment preference in eligible posts
  • Bonus marks or weightage where applicable
  • Opportunities in police and paramilitary services
  • Useful leadership and teamwork experience
🌟

Personal Development

NCC helps cadets develop discipline, confidence, leadership, communication skills and a strong sense of responsibility.

  • Leadership development
  • Self-confidence and discipline
  • Teamwork and communication
  • Sense of responsibility and service
🏔️

Adventure & Training

Cadets can participate in a variety of camps and adventure activities that encourage courage, endurance and teamwork.

  • Mountaineering
  • Rock climbing
  • Trekking and camping
  • Sailing and outdoor activities
🌍

Youth Exchange Programme

Eligible cadets may get opportunities to participate in international Youth Exchange Programmes and interact with young people from other countries.

  • International exposure
  • Cultural exchange
  • International interaction
  • Development of global awareness
🎓

Scholarships & Recognition

Eligible and meritorious cadets may receive scholarships, financial assistance or recognition through applicable NCC welfare and scholarship programmes.

  • Scholarship opportunities
  • Cadet welfare support
  • Recognition for achievements
  • Encouragement for meritorious cadets
Important: NCC benefits, special entries, vacancies, recruitment preferences and scholarship eligibility depend on the applicable service, organisation and current official notification.
